## 2. The engine

Design it yourself from first principles and public literature (for example John Chowning's 1973 paper on FM synthesis and standard DSP textbooks), citing what you used in PROVENANCE.md and DESIGN-NOTES.md. Never look at any FM-1 firmware, any other synth's firmware or any FM engine's source, and use no vendored synth code (Plaits stays in its demo).
- **Operators:** six per voice: phase accumulator, our own sine method, frequency as a ratio of the note or a fixed frequency, fine detune, output level, and self-feedback on at least one operator, with a stated strategy for keeping aliasing low at high modulation.
- **Algorithms:** a documented set of operator connections of our own design, covering stacks, parallel carriers and branches, selected by ALGORITHM. Describe each as a small diagram in the docs.
- **Envelopes:** one per operator and one for pitch: multi-stage, rate and level based, with curves you choose and justify; velocity sensitivity.
- **Playing:** polyphony with voice allocation and stealing you design; mono mode with legato and glide; pitch and glide controls; MASTER volume; smooth (zipper-free) parameter changes.
- **Two cores:** voices spread across both cores, following SR-6's guidance (atomic locks, synchronisation around shared memory), deterministic in fwtest.
- **Patches:** a small, documented, versioned patch format (for saving in M7 and sharing in the app later), and a handful of test patches of our own. No Yamaha factory voices, no copied presets. Full editing and presets come in M8; for now give a minimal, documented way to reach the engine's parameters from the controls, enough to test.

## 3. Tests

- Objective audio tests on fwtest captures: a simple two-operator patch's spectrum matches FM theory (sideband frequencies and Bessel-function amplitudes for a given index) within stated tolerances; pitch accuracy across the range; envelope timing; no clicks on note on/off or voice stealing; no underruns at full polyphony under the heaviest input script; mono/legato/glide behaviour; identical output across runs.
- Golden WAVs for the test patches.
- R-FM-* requirements, traced in the record.
